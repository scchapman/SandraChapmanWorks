Ceramics & Mixed Media

My work explores the liminal space between art and science. I find inspiration in natural forms and in their patterns that are forged in the laws of physics. I enjoy the physicality of working in clay and on canvas, and the ethereal nature of digital. My work is representational, sometimes a human hand, or a horse's head, sometimes a turbulent vortex, or a magnetic field.
